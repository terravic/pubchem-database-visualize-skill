# Globally Harmonized System (GHS) Hazard Classification Reference

## 1. Overview
The Globally Harmonized System of Classification and Labelling of Chemicals (GHS) provides an internationally agreed standard for chemical hazard communication. In PubChem, GHS classifications are curated from authoritative sources including the European Chemicals Agency (ECHA) C&L Inventory, Japanese NITE, and chemical manufacturers.

---

## 2. Signal Words
The GHS defines two hierarchy-based signal words:
- **DANGER**: Indicates severe hazard categories (e.g., fatal if swallowed, severe skin burns, flammable liquid Category 1).
- **WARNING**: Indicates less severe hazard categories (e.g., harmful if swallowed, skin irritation Category 2).
- **NOT CLASSIFIED**: Indicates that available toxicological and physical data do not meet threshold criteria for hazardous designation.

## 3. Hazard Statements (H-Codes)

### 3.1 Physical Hazards (H200 Series)
- `H225`: Highly flammable liquid and vapour.
- `H226`: Flammable liquid and vapour.
- `H272`: May intensify fire; oxidiser.
- `H290`: May be corrosive to metals.

### 3.2 Health Hazards (H300 Series)
- `H300`: Fatal if swallowed.
- `H301`: Toxic if swallowed.
- `H302`: Harmful if swallowed.
- `H314`: Causes severe skin burns and eye damage.
- `H315`: Causes skin irritation.
- `H317`: May cause an allergic skin reaction.
- `H318`: Causes serious eye damage.
- `H319`: Causes serious eye irritation.
- `H330`: Fatal if inhaled.
- `H335`: May cause respiratory irritation.
- `H350`: May cause cancer.
- `H360`: May damage fertility or the unborn child.
- `H372`: Causes damage to organs through prolonged or repeated exposure.

### 3.3 Environmental Hazards (H400 Series)
- `H400`: Very toxic to aquatic life.
- `H410`: Very toxic to aquatic life with long-lasting effects.
- `H411`: Toxic to aquatic life with long-lasting effects.

## 4. Precautionary Statements (P-Codes)
- **P100 Series (General)**: e.g., `P102` (Keep out of reach of children).
- **P200 Series (Prevention)**: e.g., `P260` (Do not breathe dust/fume/gas/mist/vapours/spray), `P280` (Wear protective gloves/clothing/eye protection).
- **P300 Series (Response)**: e.g., `P301+P310` (IF SWALLOWED: Immediately call a POISON CENTER/doctor).
- **P400 Series (Storage)**: e.g., `P405` (Store locked up).
- **P500 Series (Disposal)**: e.g., `P501` (Dispose of contents/container to approved waste facility).
