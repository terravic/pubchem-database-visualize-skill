PubChem Data Usage and Citation Acknowledgment
==============================================

This workspace acknowledges and complies with the following National Center for Biotechnology Information (NCBI) and PubChem policies:

1. PubChem Citation Guidelines:
   URL: https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/docs/citation-guidelines
   Kim S, Chen J, Cheng T, et al. PubChem 2023 update. Nucleic Acids Res. 2023;51(D1):D1373-D1380. doi:10.1093/nar/gkac956.

2. PubChem PUG-REST Usage Policy:
   URL: https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/docs/pug-rest
   - Rate limit: No more than 5 requests per second.
   - Do not submit more than 400 requests in a single minute.
   - Limit data transfer during peak hours (Monday through Friday 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM US Eastern Time).
   - Use exponential backoff upon receiving HTTP 429 (Too Many Requests) or HTTP 503 (Service Unavailable) status codes.

3. License Terms:
   PubChem data are provided as a public domain biomedical informatics resource. Users are responsible for complying with provider-specific restrictions where annotated.
